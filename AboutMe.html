About Me

‎ ‎

Who I Am

‎

I am Geronimo O. Paraan, a 16-year-old from Bulacan who loves a good laugh and never misses a chance to crack a joke. You might hear me say I'm single and ready to mingle, but that's just my playful side showing, because deep down I have a much bigger dream waiting to be chased.

‎

My true ambition is to become a seafarer, to sail across the vast oceans and see the world beyond the horizon. There is something about the sea that calls to me, a sense of adventure and purpose that I hold close as I study and prepare for the journey ahead.

‎

For now, I am happy right where I am, living a simple life that I truly cherish. I've learned that simplicity brings peace and clarity, and it is in these quiet, everyday moments that I find the strength and focus to keep working toward my dreams.

‎ ‎

My Background

‎

I grew up here in Bulacan, and we have never moved away since, this place has always been my home. My mom once worried that staying in the province might put our future at risk, or at least that is how I understood what she felt, though I've always felt right exactly where we are.

‎

In our family, I am the middle son among three siblings, the one who often finds himself in the middle of things, looking out for both the older and younger ones. Growing up, I went to Tuktukan Elementary School for my preschool and elementary years, building the foundation of my education right here in our town.

‎

As I got older, I continued my studies at Marcelo H. del Pilar National High School, and later moved on to ICI Balagtas to pursue further learning. Every school I've attended has shaped who I am today, and each step has been taken right here in the province I call home.

My Goals

‎

My biggest goal in life is to become truly successful and financially stable, to be rich in every way that matters. I don't chase wealth for fancy things or status; I have a much deeper, more meaningful reason behind it.

‎

Everything I work for is for my mom. She has worried and sacrificed so much for us over the years, and I want to reach a point where she never has to stress or worry about anything ever again.

‎

I want to give her the peace of mind and the comfortable life she deserves. Seeing her finally happy, secure, and free from all worries will be the greatest achievement I could ever ask for.
